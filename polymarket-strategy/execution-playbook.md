# Execution Playbook

## Pre-trade checklist
- [ ] Strategy conditions are met
- [ ] Market liquidity/spread acceptable
- [ ] Position size respects risk plan
- [ ] Invalidation condition is defined

## Entry steps
1. Verify condition snapshot (timestamp + screenshot optional)
2. Place initial order
3. Record fill price and size

## Management steps
1. Monitor key invalidation signals
2. Add/reduce only by predefined rules
3. Reassess after catalyst events

## Exit steps
1. Execute take-profit or stop/invalidation exit
2. Capture final PnL
3. Journal post-mortem
